# Licensing, Privacy, Security & Accessibility

## Licensing

The case-study material states that the historical trip data is public data made available by Motivate International Inc. under a license.

The project will clearly identify the original data provider and include the applicable licensing and attribution information supplied with the source.

The exact license terms should be verified before redistributing the original dataset through the portfolio repository.

## Privacy

The dataset does not contain personally identifiable information such as rider names, email addresses, or credit-card numbers.

The analysis will not attempt to identify individual riders or connect trip records to personally identifiable information.

The case-study material specifically states that privacy restrictions prevent connecting pass purchases to credit-card numbers or determining whether casual riders live within the Cyclistic service area or have purchased multiple single passes.

## Security

Raw and processed data will be treated as separate stages of the analytical workflow.

The raw dataset will be preserved locally as an immutable source where possible, while cleaning and transformation will be performed through reproducible scripts.

The GitHub repository will prioritize reproducibility and transparency rather than automatically publishing every raw data file. The decision to redistribute raw or cleaned datasets will depend on the applicable licensing terms, file size, and portfolio requirements.

## Accessibility

The final analysis will be designed for both technical and non-technical audiences.

The project will use:

- Clear titles and section headings
- Clearly defined KPIs
- Readable and purposeful visualizations
- Plain business language
- Clear explanations of what happened, why it matters, and what could be done next
- Appropriate labels and context in visualizations
- A concise README that explains the project and its business value

Technical details such as SQL queries, Python scripts, data-cleaning procedures, and Power BI implementation will remain available for readers who want to examine the methodology without overwhelming non-technical audiences.

The goal is for a recruiter, manager, or executive to understand the value of the analysis without needing to understand the underlying code.

## Overall Assessment

The dataset can be used for this portfolio analysis while respecting the available licensing and privacy information.

The project will distinguish between data transparency and unnecessary redistribution of source data, and will emphasize reproducibility, responsible data handling, and clear communication of analytical findings.
