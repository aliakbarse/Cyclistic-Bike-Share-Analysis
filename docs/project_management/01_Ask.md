# 1. ASK

## Business Problem

Cyclistic wants to increase the number of annual memberships by converting more casual riders into annual members.

## Business Task

Analyze the differences between casual riders and annual members to identify opportunities to convert casual riders into members.

## Stakeholders

Director of Marketing, Marketing Analytics Team, and Executive Team.

## Objectives

Identify behavioral patterns and characteristics of casual riders that can be used to develop strategies for converting them into annual members.

## Key Questions

1. How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently? 
2. Why would casual riders buy Cyclistic annual memberships? 
3. How can Cyclistic use digital media to influence casual riders to become members?

## Metrics

Number of rides
Average ride duration
Total ride duration
Membership revenue
Membership conversion rate 

## Success Criteria

The analysis produces clear, evidence-based findings that can support a strategy for increasing annual memberships.
